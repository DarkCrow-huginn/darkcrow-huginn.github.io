Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Birch is a budget app for iPhone and Mac made by an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what happens with your information when you use Birch. The short version: your records are yours. They stay on your device and in your own iCloud, and we never see them.

Information you enter

The amounts, merchants, categories, notes, receipt photos and subscriptions you add are stored on your device. If you use iCloud, Apple also stores them in your private iCloud database so they sync between your iPhone and Mac. That database belongs to your Apple Account, and we cannot access it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Birch has no account system and no servers of its own, and it never asks for bank logins. We don't collect, receive, sell or share your records.

Receipt scanning and the camera

Birch uses the camera only when you choose to scan a receipt. Text recognition (reading the store, date, total and items) runs on your device using Apple's built-in technology. Receipt photos are not uploaded anywhere by Birch. They are stored with your records as described above.

Face ID and Touch ID

If you turn on the lock in Settings, Apple's system checks your face or fingerprint. Birch only learns whether unlocking succeeded. It never receives biometric data.

Purchases

Birch Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment. We don't receive your payment details. Birch only checks with Apple whether your subscription is active.

Advertising (free version on iPhone)

The free version of Birch on iPhone shows a small banner ad provided by Google AdMob. Birch Pro and the Mac app show no ads.

  • Birch asks Google for non-personalized ads only.
  • Birch does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not shared.
  • To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your IP address (and an approximate location derived from it), device and app information, and how you interact with ads.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choices before ads are shown.
  • Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your financial records are never shared with Google or any advertiser.

Exports

When you export a Tax Summary, the PDF and CSV files are created on your device. They go only where you choose to share or save them, for example an email to your accountant.

Deleting your data

  • In Birch: Settings ▸ Delete all data removes all records, receipt photos, subscriptions and custom categories.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from that device.
  • Data synced through iCloud can be managed in your device's iCloud settings.

Children

Birch is a general budgeting app and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
