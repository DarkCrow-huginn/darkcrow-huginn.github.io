Birch

Spending Log, Receipt Scanner · iPhone and Mac

A calm budget app for your daily spending and a small side business. Simple enough to understand in three seconds, with clean numbers for your accountant at the end of the year.

What Birch does

  • See where this month's money went in one calm chart, and what's left of your budget.
  • Add a record in seconds, or scan a receipt. Mixed receipts (milk and shampoo) are split by category automatically.
  • Birch suggests categories and learns from your changes.
  • Compare months over half a year or a full year.
  • Track side-business income, expenses and software subscriptions.
  • Export a Tax Summary (PDF + CSV) for your accountant with Birch Pro.
  • Private by design: your records stay on your device and in your own iCloud. No account, no bank login.

Help

How do I add a record?

Tap the round + button (on Mac: ⌘N). Type the amount, tap Next, add where you spent it, and tap Save.

How does receipt scanning work?

Tap + and then Scan receipt. Birch reads the store, date, total and items on your device. If a receipt mixes categories, you can check the split before saving. The photo is kept with the record.

How do I export the Tax Summary?

Open Business, choose the year, and tap Export Tax Summary. You get a PDF and a CSV of your business income and expenses by category to send to your accountant. This is part of Birch Pro.

Does Birch sync between iPhone and Mac?

Yes, through iCloud. Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, on both devices.

How do I restore or cancel Birch Pro?

To restore: Settings ▸ Restore purchases in Birch. To cancel on iPhone: Settings app ▸ your name ▸ Subscriptions ▸ Birch Pro. On Mac: App Store ▸ your name ▸ Account Settings ▸ Subscriptions.

How do I delete my data?

In Birch: Settings ▸ Delete all data. Deleting the app also removes the data on that device.

Is Birch tax advice?

No. Birch summarizes the records you enter. Please talk to your accountant or tax professional about your situation.
